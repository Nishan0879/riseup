Questions owners ask

Do my customers need an app?

No. Most modern iPhones and Android phones read the card automatically. They tap, and your review page opens in their browser.

Is asking for reviews this way allowed?

Yes. Asking customers for honest reviews is fine. Just don't offer discounts or gifts in exchange for a review, and ask everyone, not only the happy customers.

How fast can I get a card?

Text or call me with your business name. I'll set the card up with your review link and bring it to you.

Can I get more than one?

Yes. Many businesses put one at the register and one on tables or by the door. Ask me about a price for more than one.
