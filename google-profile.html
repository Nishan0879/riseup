Questions owners ask

Do I have to give you my Google password?

No. You add me as a manager on your profile. I can make changes, you stay the owner, and you can remove me anytime.

What if I don't have a profile yet?

I'll create it with you. Google needs to verify that the business is yours, usually by video, phone or mail, so you'll need to be there for that step.

Will this put me at the top of Google Maps?

Nobody can promise a ranking. A complete, accurate profile with good photos and steady reviews gives you the best chance to show up and get picked.

How long does it take?

Most tune-ups are done in one visit. Some changes take Google a few days to show.
